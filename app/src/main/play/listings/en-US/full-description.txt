Hogwarts Academy - The Smart School Companion

Stay connected with your school like never before. Hogwarts Academy brings your entire school experience to your fingertips.

FOR STUDENTS:
- View your class schedule and timetable
- Check grades, exam results, and GPA
- Track attendance records and submit excuses
- Pay school fees online
- Receive instant notifications for announcements

FOR TEACHERS:
- Mark attendance with manual entry, bulk marking, or QR codes
- Submit grades and assessment scores
- Communicate with parents and guardians
- View class schedules and student lists

FOR PARENTS & GUARDIANS:
- Monitor your child's academic progress
- Track attendance and receive absence alerts
- View and pay school fees
- Communicate directly with teachers

KEY FEATURES:
- Offline support - Access your data even without internet
- Multi-language - Available in English and Arabic (RTL)
- Secure - Biometric login, encrypted data, certificate pinning
- Beautiful UI - Apple-inspired design with glassmorphism effects
- Real-time notifications - Never miss an important update
- Multi-school support - Switch between schools seamlessly
